# Protean engineering

Read [docs/tiger-style.md](docs/tiger-style.md) before changing Zig code. It owns
the TigerStyle adaptation, mechanical gate, and review obligations that the
auditor cannot establish.

Preserve checked authoring, model and tool admission, execution custody, durable
recovery, and the independent qualification described in
[.github/CI.md](.github/CI.md). Style adoption does not authorize narrowing
supported inputs or weakening verification. Do not add explanatory comments in
code, tests, or examples; express intent through names, types, and operations.
